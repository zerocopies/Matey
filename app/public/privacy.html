Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

Overview

Matey is a privacy-first application. Your personal data, journal entries, My-VOTS responses, photos, and AI conversations stay on your device. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your data in any way.

What Is NOT Collected

The following data is never seen, stored, or transmitted to any server operated by the Matey developer:

  • Journal entries — all content, titles, tags, and attachments
  • My-VOTS entries — all response content, source links, and attachments
  • Wardrobe/Grooming/Living photos — all captured or selected images
  • BYOK API keys — your OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or other provider credentials
  • File contents from Agent DKI's workspace
  • Any content processed through your user-configured AI providers
  • Usage analytics, behavioral tracking, or session recordings

What IS Collected

Anonymous crash/error reports via Sentry Crash Reporting Only

When the app crashes or encounters an unhandled error, we collect anonymous technical data to help fix the issue:

  • Stack traces and error messages
  • Device type and model (e.g., "Pixel 8", "iPhone 15 Pro")
  • Operating system version (e.g., "Android 15", "iOS 18.1")
  • App version number

This data is anonymized at collection time. Sentry is configured with sendDefaultPii: false and all sensitive data is scrubbed before transmission.

BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) Disclosure

When you connect your own AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, etc.) for features like Grooming, Wardrobe, Living, or Agent DKI:

  • Your data is sent directly from your device to that provider using your own credentials
  • The Matey developer is never part of that data flow
  • The Matey developer never has access to your API keys or the data sent to your provider
  • All provider interactions happen over encrypted HTTPS connections

Local Storage

All personal data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Journal entries, My-VOTS entries, tags, and covers are stored in `localStorage` and `IndexedDB` on your device
  • Photos and attachments are stored in local device storage
  • No cloud backup is provided by the app
  • You may optionally back up entries manually to your own cloud storage (e.g., Google Drive, iCloud) at your own discretion

No Analytics

Matey does not include any analytics SDK (Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.). No usage, behavioral, or session tracking of any kind is performed.
